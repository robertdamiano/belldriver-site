About Us

Shaping confident drivers since 1954

Welcome to Bell Driver Education Inc.

Where Excellence Drives Success!

Embark on your journey to becoming a confident and responsible driver with Bell Driver, the trusted choice for over 70 years. We are proud to be a Ministry of Transportation approved driving school serving the Greater Toronto and York Region.

Our Team

Experienced professionals dedicated to your success on the road.

Administrative Team

Our administrative team oversees all operations at Bell Driver Education, ensuring that every client receives a seamless, supportive experience—from initial inquiries through registration and the certification process.

In-Class Instructors

Our in-class instructors bring years of Canadian driving experience and expertise, along with a genuine passion for teaching. They are dedicated to guiding students through the theoretical knowledge required, while fostering the confidence and mindset needed to become safe, defensive drivers.

In-Vehicle Instructors

Our in-vehicle instructors are dedicated to preparing students with the skills needed for everyday driving, as well as handling the many emergencies they may encounter throughout their driving experience. We instill the right attitude, awareness, and abilities in every student, equipping them with the knowledge and confidence to become safe, responsible, and highly skilled drivers.

Passing the Torch of Success!

Discover how Bell Driver has shaped the driving journeys of thousands. Envision yourself cruising down the road with confidence, just like our 75,000+ successful drivers. We strive to develop in every student a desire to be an AAA driver.
