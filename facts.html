Your Journey to a Driver's Licence

Three steps to becoming a fully licensed Ontario driver.

1

I don't have a G1 yet — should I take this course?

Starting driver education before getting your G1 has real advantages. Our in-class instruction gives you the knowledge to pass your G1 written test with confidence. You can complete the course while working toward your G1, so you're fully prepared when you get behind the wheel.

2

I'm done my 10-hour in-car lessons — what's next?

Congratulations! Once you've completed your 10 hours of in-vehicle instruction, you'll receive your course completion certificate. You can then book your G2 road test — and if you completed a ministry-approved course like ours, you qualify after just 8 months (instead of 12).

3

I passed my road test — what should I do after?

Well done! After passing your G2 road test, continue building your skills and experience. Drive in a variety of conditions, always wear your seatbelt, and remember the AAA principles: Awareness, Ability, and Attitude. Your full G licence awaits in 12 months (with a highway road test).
