In-Class Program

Our Ministry-approved driver education program prepares students for G1, G2, and G (Highway) tests.

Includes 20 hours of classroom instruction using the See Think Do curriculum, available on weekdays, weekends, or Saturdays.

Online Program

Contact us to register — we set up your access to the MTO-approved online platform, then you complete the course at your own pace.

In-Vehicle Training

Includes 10 hours of in-vehicle instruction focused on defensive driving and an introduction to highway driving.

Lessons can be booked Monday to Sunday with pickup and drop-off from home or school within the Greater Toronto and York Region.

Homelink

Both in-class and online programs include 10 hours of homelink assignments to reinforce your learning and apply it to real world situations.

Certification

When course requirements have been completed, students will be electronically certified for a Driver's Licence History (DLH) and will be ready for request at any Ministry of Transportation driver and vehicle licencing office (Service Ontario). Completion of a ministry-approved program may qualify students for an insurance rate reduction.
